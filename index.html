How to Play

Move your bucket to catch clean water droplets.

  • Use the arrow keys or A and D to move.
  • Clean water earns you 1 point.
  • Dirty water costs you 2 points.
  • Catch clean water to reach 20 points and win before time runs out.
  • Your streak counts consecutive clean droplets caught.

Every Drop Matters

Access to clean water supports health, education, and oppurtunity. Charity: water works to bring clean and safe drinking water to communities around the world.

Small actions can help raise awareness of the global water crisis.
